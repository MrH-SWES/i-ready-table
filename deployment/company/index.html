Math Things edTech

Math, in your hands.

Digital mathematical objects for teachers and students to move, split, join, compare, measure, and build. The teacher teaches. Math Things gives the classroom something to explore.

Open Teaching Table

A workspace for classroom mathematics

Teaching Table brings Math Things manipulatives, written responses, local curriculum files, and worksheet display together in one classroom workspace. It supports PDF and EPUB materials, read-aloud tools, and phone-to-table worksheet uploads.

Use curriculum materials you are licensed to access. Local books and settings remain in the browser where you added them; uploading a worksheet uses the Teaching Table relay service.
